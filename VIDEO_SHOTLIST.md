## Capture and edit

Use readable text at the actual recording resolution. Zoom into a few useful lines instead of rapidly scrolling a terminal. Long check runs may be cut with an **Elapsed time omitted** label; keep the real result and timestamps. Avoid blending scratch fixtures into the real repository narrative.

Use the Author's own narration or captions. If generated narration is requested, use Deepgram only after credentials and available credits are confirmed; do not substitute another provider or buy credits. Use no copyrighted music, jump scares or horror clips. The UI's original artwork is sufficient.
