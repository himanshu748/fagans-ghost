# Fagan history and citations

## Verified

- Fagan, M. E. (1976). "Design and code inspections to reduce errors in program development." *IBM Systems Journal* 15(3), 182-211. [DOI 10.1147/sj.153.0182](https://doi.org/10.1147/sj.153.0182).

This is the only verified citation so far. Cite it as above.

## Needs citation

Do not state any of these as fact. Either leave them out or write them with the marker "(needs citation)":

- Any defect detection rate, error-removal percentage or productivity gain attributed to inspections.
- Recommended preparation and inspection rates in LOC per hour.
- Recommended team size and meeting length.
- Which role names (Moderator, Reader, Inspectors, Author, Scribe) appear in the 1976 paper and which come from later sources such as Fagan's 1986 paper or IEEE 1028.
- Whether Planning is a named phase in 1976 or was added later.
- Fagan's defect type codes and the missing, wrong or extra classification.
- Any reinspection threshold.
- The claim that formal inspection "mostly died out", and the reasons.
